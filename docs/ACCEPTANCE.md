# Acceptance audit

Checked against the running app at `http://localhost:3000` on demo date 14 Nov 2026, and against the unit tests named below. Demo data was reset at the end. Every row passed. No product code needed a change.

Opening a household shows every eligible product term (H01: HELOAN Yellow 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30-year, and HELOAN Pink 20 and 30-year), plus the current path, avalanche and snowball. That is the "one scenario per eligible product" row: each offer from the catalogue is on screen, so a term that raises the payment stays visible.
